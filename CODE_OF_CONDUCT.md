# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone. Be
constructive, assume good faith, discuss technical decisions rather than people,
and protect private or security-sensitive information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private data.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may
remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. This
policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
